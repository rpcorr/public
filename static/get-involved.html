Get Involved

People who stutter deserve to be heard, understood, and supported in every part of life. By getting involved with the Newfoundland and Labrador Stuttering Association, you can help create a more inclusive and compassionate community. Whether you choose to participate in research, volunteer your time, attend events, or simply learn more, your involvement makes a meaningful difference—for individuals, families, and the future of communication in our province.

We Need Your Voice

Help shape research that improves access and support for people who stutter in Newfoundland and Labrador.

Ways you can get involved:

  • Surveys: Share your experiences
  • Interviews: Tell your story
  • Community conversations: Learn and connect
  • Peer support: Join our community

Your participation makes a real difference. Let's work together to create positive change!
